Profile

Jacob Falkentorp is a senior IT architect and independent advisor with some thirty years in Danish central-government IT transformation (UFST, the Agency for Public Finance and Management). He takes on concrete IT work as a finished delivery: analysis, architecture, technical requirements, vendor selection, and sound operation, on his own or alongside the client's own people.

He covers the whole arc, from analysis and needs assessment, through data model and IT architecture and technical requirements, to price estimation and vendor selection, among them the selection of the top six vendors for a central-government framework agreement worth DKK 4 billion. He advises vendor-neutrally on the choice of solution model (open source versus proprietary).

As a freelance and interim consultant he works as an independent third party, with no ties to vendors or products. He is at home with public IT tenders, database build-out and data exposure, and the requirements of GDPR and information security. He gets up to speed quickly in a new field.
