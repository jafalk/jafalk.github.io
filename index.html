Consultant profile

Jacob Falkentorp is an experienced IT architect and independent advisor with around 30 years of experience and deep roots in Danish government IT transformation (Danish Tax Agency, Danish Agency for Public Finance and Management). He solves concrete IT assignments as a complete deliverable: analysis, architecture, technical requirements, vendor selection and responsible operation, alone or together with the client's own people.

He works holistically - seeing architecture, cloud, security and the people who will use the solution as one picture - and is strongest where domains meet: in heterogeneous, hybrid system landscapes across on-premises and cloud and several vendors. He covers the full course from analysis and needs assessment, through data model, IT architecture and technical requirements, to cost estimation and vendor selection, including selecting the top six vendors for a DKK 4 billion government framework agreement.

As a freelancer or interim, he works as an independent third party with no ties to vendors or products. He advises vendor-neutrally on the choice of solution model (open source versus proprietary) and deliberately builds exit routes into the architecture so the client is not locked in. Experienced with public IT tenders, building databases and exposing data, GDPR and information security. He gets up to speed quickly in new business domains.
